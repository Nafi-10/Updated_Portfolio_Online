# Sample Article: Exploring AI and IoT Convergence

*By MD Kazi Jawadul Islam*

As a student in Computing & Information Systems with a major in Artificial Intelligence, the convergence of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and the Internet of Things (IoT) presents one of the most exciting frontiers in modern computer science.

## What is AIoT?

AIoT (Artificial Intelligence of Things) is the combination of AI technologies with IoT infrastructure to achieve more efficient IoT operations, improve human-machine interactions, and enhance data management and analytics.

### Key Pillars:
1. **Edge Intelligence**: Processing sensor data directly on localized microcontrollers or gateways.
2. **Predictive Analytics**: Machine learning models anticipating device failures or system loads.
3. **Automated Cyber Defense**: Real-time anomaly detection protecting network nodes against intrusion.
